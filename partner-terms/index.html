World Partner terms

Version of 30 October 2026

These terms are for businesses buying a World Partner placement on app.fistbump.world/sponsor. The seller is Sinda Khenine (Fistbump), a sole proprietor, Rothenburg ob der Tauber, Germany (contact@skatalystai.com). Placements are sold to businesses only, not to consumers.

What you buy

A labelled advertising placement on the Fistbump member map for one territory: a country (Compact, Standard or Prominent), one of the nine regions, or the single Global spot. Your logo is marked “Sponsored” and opens your business profile in Fistbump, with one “Visit website” button. It shows on the web and in the iPhone and Android apps.

“Exclusive” means only the placement itself: while you hold a territory, no other business can buy the same territory. It doesn’t keep other businesses off Fistbump, out of other territories, or stop members from mentioning anyone.

How often your logo shows depends on where members look. Up to 6 logos show at once on a computer and 3 on a phone, taking turns when more qualify. People, trips and meetups always come first: where there’s no free spot, your logo stays hidden. We don’t promise views, clicks or any result.

Approval and payment

We review every application by hand and may decline it without giving reasons. Nothing is charged before we approve it. Once approved, your territory is held for you for the time shown on your private link (72 hours unless we say otherwise), and the placement starts when Stripe confirms your payment.

A placement is a subscription billed monthly or every 3 months in advance, at the price shown when we approved you. Prices are net prices. Whether VAT is added depends on our VAT status and where your business is; Stripe’s payment page shows it before you pay, and your invoice states it. There is no setup fee; changes to your logo, profile and link, and the monthly report, are included. The first ten World Partners keep their price for six months and get one launch post from the Fistbump account on X, with no promised reach.

Cancelling, failed payments, ending

You can cancel at any time from the billing page in your private link or by email. Cancelling stops the next renewal; your logo stays until the end of the period you already paid for, and that period isn’t refunded. If a payment fails, your logo comes off the map until it is paid; if it still isn’t paid after Stripe’s retries, the placement ends and the territory becomes free.

We may hide or remove a logo or profile that is misleading or unlawful, or that advertises gambling, adult content, weapons, tobacco, political campaigns or anything else our members wouldn’t expect on Fistbump. If we end a placement for a reason that isn’t yours, we refund the unused part of the period.

Your content

You confirm you may use the name, logo, pictures, links and words you send us, and that your offer to members is true. You allow us to show them on Fistbump while your placement runs, and in our own posts about World Partners.

Reports and privacy

Each month we report your placement’s days live, views (at least half of your logo on screen for one second), profile opens and website clicks. Hidden browser tabs, logos covered by people or by the app, our own previews and anyone not signed in are not counted. You get totals only, never who saw your logo. How we handle your own details is in our privacy policy.

The Season

World Partners don’t sponsor the Fistbump Season and have no say in it. Fistbump runs the Season alone, and entry is free.

Law

German law applies. Our general terms apply to everything else.
