World Partner terms

Version of 10 November 2026. It applies to offers made under it; the current terms apply to every offer since, and the version of 5 November 2026 to placements approved before 10 November.

These terms are for businesses buying a World Partner placement on app.fistbump.world/sponsor. The seller is Sinda Khenine (Fistbump), a sole proprietor, Rothenburg ob der Tauber, Germany (contact@skatalystai.com). Placements are sold to businesses only, not to consumers.

What you buy

A labelled advertising placement on the Fistbump member map for one territory: a country, one of the nine regions, or the whole map (Global). Your land on the map takes your colour, light and see-through, and your logo is drawn once inside it, with a “Sponsored” label that opens your business profile in Fistbump, with one “Visit website” button. It shows on the web and in the iPhone and Android apps, at every zoom until street level, where every logo fades out. People, trips, meetups and place names are always drawn on top of it. Your logo keeps its colours and proportions and is never stretched.

Your land is the territory less what another business already holds for your dates when your offer is made, and less the areas you ask us to leave out (at most two). Each piece of the map is sold to one business at a time: a country inside a region, or a region inside the whole map, held by someone else isn’t part of your placement, and land you hold is never sold to anyone else while your placement runs. The territories and their borders are the ones the map draws (coverage version 2026-11-v2); Europe is geographic Europe, with Russia, Kazakhstan and Türkiye only west of the Urals, the Ural River and the Bosporus.

“Exclusive” means your land: no other business can buy any of it for your dates. It doesn’t cover member profiles, company logos on members’ characters, other land, or what members post or mention.

Price

The price depends on the size of the land, never on members or audience. Each territory has a full price for its whole area, from one published schedule for every country, region and the whole map (the larger the area, the higher the price). When part of a country or a region is held by someone else, the land that is yours is priced by its own size on the same schedule. For the whole map, each country (or other piece of land) another business has paid for takes a fixed amount off the full price, never more than that country’s own price and never more than half of the full price in all; land you leave out yourself doesn’t lower the price. Areas are worked out in square kilometres from the land the map draws. Your offer shows the full price, the land already booked, the land that is yours, and your price, before you accept it.

Prices are every 3 months, in US dollars. Prices are net prices. Whether VAT is added depends on our VAT status and where your business is; Stripe’s payment page shows it before you pay, and your invoice states it. There is no setup fee; changes to your logo, profile and link, and the monthly report, are included. The first ten World Partners keep their price for six months and get one launch post from the Fistbump account on X, with no promised reach.

Offer, acceptance and payment

We review every application by hand and may decline it without giving reasons. Nothing is charged before we approve it. With the approval we send an offer to your private link: the territory, your land and what is left out, the price, the currency and the term. We hold your land while the offer is open (72 hours unless we say otherwise). You accept it by name on your private link; then you pay it by card through Stripe. The offer can’t change after we make it: a new price or other land only comes as a new offer you accept. We check the offer again before Stripe takes the payment, and only a payment of the offer you accepted starts your placement.

A placement starts on the day Stripe confirms your payment and runs 3 months, renewing every 3 months until you cancel, at the price and on the land of your offer.

If we can’t show your placement for reasons on our side for more than 24 hours in total in one term, we add the days lost to your term at no cost.

Cancelling, failed payments, ending

You can cancel at any time from the billing page in your private link or by email. Payments already made are not refunded. Cancelling stops the next renewal; your logo stays until the end of the period you paid for. If a payment fails, your logo comes off the map until it is paid; if it still isn’t paid after Stripe’s retries, the placement ends and the territory becomes free.

We may hide or remove a logo or profile that is misleading or unlawful, or that advertises gambling, adult content, weapons, tobacco, political campaigns or anything else our members wouldn’t expect on Fistbump.

One correction: if a payment reaches us after we released your hold and your land is held by another business by then, or for another offer than the one you accepted, or twice, we cancel that subscription and refund that payment in full.

Your content

You confirm you may use the name, logo, pictures, links and words you send us, and that your offer to members is true. You allow us to show them on Fistbump while your placement runs, and in our own posts about World Partners.

Reports and privacy

Each month we report your placement’s days live, views (at least half of your logo on the map for one second, fully shown at that zoom), profile opens and website clicks. Your private link also shows this month’s numbers so far and last month’s, updated once a day. Hidden browser tabs, logos still fading in or out, logos behind the app, our own previews, our team, test and review accounts, more than one view per member every half hour and anyone not signed in are not counted. We don’t report how many different people saw your logo, as that would mean keeping who saw it, and we don’t promise views, clicks, sales or any other result. Examples are always marked as such. You get totals only, never who saw your logo. How we handle your own details is in our privacy policy.

The Season
