Delete your Fistbump account

Fistbump by Sinda Khenine

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Fistbump (the app or app.fistbump.world) and sign in.
  2. Tap your picture (top right), then 🛡️ Privacy.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Type your @handle to confirm. Your account is deleted at once.

Still waiting for approval? Tap your picture, then Customize me, and you find Delete my account at the bottom.

By email

If you can’t sign in, write to contact@skatalystai.com with your X @handle. We confirm that the request comes from you and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your profile, character, profile photo, X data we imported, follow checks, trips, events you host, pings, invitations, messages, notifications, Fistbumps, meeting photos, Season entry, moods, Fistbump Park games and ladder, and device tokens. If you have Fistbump Plus, the subscription is cancelled when the account is deleted.

What is kept, and for how long

Reports other members made about an account may be kept as long as needed to handle them. Payment records (invoices) are kept by Stripe and us for as long as tax law requires, usually 10 years in Germany.

Deleting only some data

Without deleting your account you can remove a meeting photo, cancel trips and events, leave the Season board, hide yourself from the map, leave Fistbump news, clear your profile text, and download a copy of your data (your picture → 🛡️ Privacy → Download my data).
