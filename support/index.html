Contact us

Write to contact@skatalystai.com about your account, a bug, a payment or anything else. If it is about your account, add your X @handle.

Report or block someone

  • Report a person: tap them on the map to open their profile, tap 🛡️ Report & block, then Report, and tell us what happened.
  • Report something they posted: in a conversation, on an event or on a trip, tap 🚩 Report and pick a reason.
  • Block: on the same tab, tap Block. You stop seeing each other on Fistbump and open requests close. To undo it, tap your picture (top right), then 🛡️ Privacy, and Unblock.
  • Mute: no more notifications about that person. Everything else stays.

Nobody is told when you mute, block or report them. We review every report within 24 hours. Fistbump has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users: when something breaks our terms, we remove the content and remove the account that posted it.

A concern about a child

Read our child safety standards and write to founder@skatalystai.com, whether or not you have a Fistbump account. If a child is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number (112 in Europe, 911 in the US) first.

Delete your account

  1. Open Fistbump and sign in.
  2. Tap your picture (top right), then 🛡️ Privacy.
  3. Tap Delete my account and type your @handle to confirm.

Your account is deleted at once. If you can’t sign in, or want to know what is deleted, see Delete your account.

Your data

To get a copy of your data, tap your picture (top right), then 🛡️ Privacy and Download my data. How we handle your data is in the privacy policy.
