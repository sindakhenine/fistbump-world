The service

Fistbump helps builders who know each other on X meet in real life. It is run by Sinda Khenine, a sole proprietor in Rothenburg ob der Tauber, Germany (contact@skatalystai.com; full details in the legal notice). Fistbump is the name of the service, not a separate company. Fistbump is a beta: features can change, and the service can be unavailable at times.

Who can join

You need to be at least 18 and sign in with your own X account. Fistbump is a curated community for builders, so we review sign-ups and may decline or remove accounts. Paying for Fistbump Plus never skips this review.

Meeting people

Fistbump only helps you arrange meetings; the people you meet are responsible for themselves. Meet in public places, tell someone where you’re going, and leave if something feels wrong. Never share a home address in an announcement or event. You can block and report anyone (see “Zero tolerance” below).

What’s not allowed

  • harassment, threats, hate, or sexual content directed at anyone;
  • spam, selling, or sending the same request to many people;
  • pretending to be someone else, or using someone else’s X account;
  • collecting other members’ data, or trying to get around privacy settings or limits;
  • illegal, violent or sexually explicit content, and anything that sexualises minors (see our child safety standards).

Zero tolerance: Fistbump has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. You can report a person from their profile, and report a conversation, an event or a trip. We review every report within 24 hours. When something breaks these rules, we remove the content and remove the account that posted it.

We may also limit or suspend accounts that break these rules.

How we moderate

  • Before anyone joins: the Fistbump team reviews every sign-up by hand.
  • Automatic checks: a word filter stops abusive words in profiles, requests, messages, trips, events, game chat and World Partner profiles, and game chat allows no links. It only stops the text from being saved; it never decides about an account.
  • Reports: members report a person, a conversation, an event, a trip or a game message in the app; anyone, also without an account, can report illegal content by email (see Support). A person on the team reads every report within 24 hours.
  • What we can do: remove content, hide or remove a profile, decline or remove an account, and pass information to the authorities where the law requires it.
  • If you disagree with a decision about your content or account, write to contact@skatalystai.com within six months; a person on the team looks at it again.

Fistbumps

A Fistbump counts once for each person you meet, only after both of you confirm the meeting. Points can’t be bought, and following someone, paying, attending an event or playing games never adds points.

Fistbump Park

The games in Fistbump Park are just for fun and never count for the Season. Until 10 November 2026 all of them, the six Plus games too, are free for everyone; after that the Plus games need Plus. An invitation to play expires after 10 minutes for live games and after a day for turn games. If you leave a game that has really started, or don’t play your turn for 3 days, it counts as a loss for you. A game’s chat has quick phrases and short typed messages, and voice if the players join it; the rules above apply to games like everywhere else. Any message can be reported, and Block in the chat ends both the messages and the voice between two players.
