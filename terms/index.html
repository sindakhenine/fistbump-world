The service

Fistbump helps builders who know each other on X meet in real life. It is run by Sinda Khenine, Rothenburg ob der Tauber, Germany (contact@skatalystai.com). Fistbump is a beta: features can change, and the service can be unavailable at times.

Who can join

You need to be at least 18 and sign in with your own X account. Fistbump is a curated community for builders, so we review sign-ups and may decline or remove accounts. Paying for Fistbump Plus never skips this review.

Meeting people

Fistbump only helps you arrange meetings; the people you meet are responsible for themselves. Meet in public places, tell someone where you’re going, and leave if something feels wrong. Never share a home address in an announcement or event. You can block and report anyone, and we act on reports.

What’s not allowed

  • harassment, threats, hate, or sexual content directed at anyone;
  • spam, selling, or sending the same request to many people;
  • pretending to be someone else, or using someone else’s X account;
  • collecting other members’ data, or trying to get around privacy settings or limits.

We may limit, suspend or remove accounts that break these rules.

Fistbumps

A Fistbump counts once for each person you meet, only after both of you confirm the meeting. Points can’t be bought, and following someone, paying, or attending an event never adds points.

Fistbump Plus

During the pilot, approved members get Fistbump Plus free until 31 December 2026. When paid plans start, prices, limits, and what ends if you stop paying will be shown before you buy, and nothing turns into a paid subscription without you choosing it. Conversations you already started and events you host stay manageable after Plus ends.

Ending

You can stop using Fistbump and delete your account at any time. We may end the service with reasonable notice.
