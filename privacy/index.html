Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Who is responsible

Fistbump is run by Sinda Khenine, Rothenburg ob der Tauber, Germany. Email: contact@skatalystai.com. This is the controller for the personal data described here under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

What we collect and why

When you sign in with X, X shares with us, with your permission:

  • your X account ID, name, @handle, profile photo and bio;
  • your follower and following counts, account creation date and the location text on your profile;
  • your email address, if X provides it;
  • whether you and other Fistbump members follow each other on X, checked only for people who are themselves Fistbump members.

We use this to create your account, show your profile, and place the right people on your map (contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We never post, follow, or send messages on X for you, and we don’t collect data about people who aren’t Fistbump members.

What you add: the country and optional city you choose (never a street address or GPS), your character’s look, interests, bio, project details, who may find you, trips and free times you announce, pings, invitations, messages, meetup confirmations, events and RSVPs, blocks and reports. We use these to run the service you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Notifications: we keep a list of updates for you (for example someone wants to meet, said yes, wrote to you, or is visiting your country). If you turn on notifications, we store your browser’s push address or your phone app’s push token so we can deliver them, and delete it when you turn them off (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

The Season: if you join the Season competition, your name, photo, country and points are shown to other members on the Season board, as described in the Season rules. Points are counted from meetings both people confirmed. When you join, you tell us where you’d like to travel and when; only the Fistbump team sees this, and sponsors see only totals of three or more. We may check the leaders’ accounts, meetings and live photos before announcing a winner, to keep the competition fair (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

Meeting photos: a photo you add of a meeting is stored privately and shown only to the two of you, and on your profiles only if you both agree; either of you can withdraw that. We remove location and other metadata from the picture before it is uploaded. Live photos taken with the in-app camera record the pose you were asked for and the time. Photos are deleted with your account (Art. 6(1)(a) and (b) GDPR).

Payments: if you buy Fistbump Plus, Stripe processes your payment. We receive your Stripe customer ID, your plan, its status and when it renews or ends, never your card details (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; invoices are kept as tax law requires, Art. 6(1)(c)).

Usage: we note when you last opened Fistbump, to count active members, and the app shows how many builders are online right now as a single number, without saying who.

Safety: reports and blocks are used to keep members safe and to act on abuse (legitimate interests, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

A profile picture you upload on the map stays in your own browser and is not sent to us.

Who can see what

Only approved members see the member map, and only according to your settings: by default just your X connections who use Fistbump, or also other approved builders if you allow it, or nobody if you hide. Your location is shown at country or city level only. Messages are visible only to the two people in the conversation. Exact event venues are shown only to guests who are going, unless the host marks the venue as a public place.

Sponsors who support Fistbump can sign in to a sponsor page. They see only counts: how many builders there are, rough regions rounded to about 100 km, popular interests and destinations. Groups of fewer than three builders are left out and members who chose to be hidden are not counted on the map. Sponsors never see names, photos, handles, messages or exact places.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores the database and handles sign-in, hosted in the EU (Frankfurt). Our X access tokens are stored encrypted.
  • Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Ireland) processes payments for Fistbump Plus under its own privacy policy.
  • Your browser’s push service (for example Google, Apple or Mozilla) delivers notifications to browsers, and Expo (650 Industries, USA) delivers them to the iPhone and Android app. Notification contents are encrypted for browsers.
  • X Corp. provides sign-in and the profile and follow data above, under X’s own privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages serves this website and the web app, and OpenFreeMap serves the map tiles. Both receive your IP address when your browser loads files from them.

Some of these providers may process data outside the EU; where they do, they rely on the EU’s standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.

Cookies and storage

We don’t use advertising or analytics cookies. Your browser stores your sign-in session and, if you upload one, your profile picture, so the app keeps working between visits.

How long we keep data

We keep your account data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, requests, messages, announcements and events are deleted; reports about other members may be kept as long as needed to handle them.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, a copy in a portable format, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interests. Write to contact@skatalystai.com. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA).

You can disconnect Fistbump from your X account at any time in X’s settings under connected apps.
