Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

Who is responsible

Fistbump is run by Sinda Khenine, Rothenburg ob der Tauber, Germany. Email: contact@skatalystai.com. This is the controller for the personal data described here under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

What we collect and why

When you sign in with X, X shares with us, with your permission:

  • your X account ID, name, @handle, profile photo and bio;
  • your follower and following counts, account creation date and the location text on your profile;
  • your email address, if X provides it;
  • whether you and other Fistbump members follow each other on X, checked only for people who are themselves Fistbump members.

We use this to create your account, show your profile, and place the right people on your map (contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We never post, follow, or send messages on X for you, and we don’t collect data about people who aren’t Fistbump members.

Your X connections: for each pair of Fistbump members, we store whether each of them follows the other on X and when we last checked. We check again every few days, or when you tap Update from X. We never read or store your full list of followers or people you follow. We use these connections to decide who appears on your map and who can find you, and to show how you know each other (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

What you add: the country and optional city you choose (never a street address or GPS), your character’s look, interests, bio, project details, who may find you, trips and free times you announce, pings, invitations, messages, meetup confirmations, events and RSVPs, blocks and reports. We use these to run the service you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Profile photo: if you upload your own profile photo, it is stored in Fistbump’s photo storage (Supabase, Frankfurt) and shown, like your X photo, to the members who can see you, for example on the map, on your profile and on the Season board. Like your X photo, it has a public web address, so anyone who has that address can open it. It is deleted when you change it or delete your account (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Moods and moves: you can give your character a mood (for example happy or coffee) or a move (for example dance or say hi). Members who can see you on the map see it until it runs out, after 15 minutes to 24 hours. You pick from a fixed list, with no free text, and we keep only your latest choice (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Who you met: your number of Fistbumps (confirmed meetings) is shown to the members who can see you. Who you met stays private: only you and the person you met see it. If you both turn on Show who I’ve fistbumped, members who can see both of you see that you two met, on your profiles. Either of you can turn it off at any time, and it never shows to people you blocked or who blocked you (Art. 6(1)(a) and (b) GDPR).

Fistbump news: if you turn on Show my Fistbumps in Fistbump news, members who can see you on the map are shown, for 30 days, that you have been meeting builders. They never see with whom, on which day or where. It is off unless you turn it on, and never shown to people you blocked or who blocked you. You find both settings under your picture → Customize me → Privacy (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

Fistbump Park (games on the website and in the iPhone and Android apps): when you play, we store each game (who played, the rules picked, the moves and scores needed to run it, the result, and why it ended if it ended early, for example that someone left), your ladder points, tier, wins, losses, draws and streaks for each game and for all games, and a history of how your points changed, also from games against the computer. The people in a game see that game, and the quick reactions sent in it (a fixed list of phrases, no free text). Members who can see you on the map can see your ladder points and record. Only you see your history and your record against your connections, grouped by how you know each other on X and person by person. Inviting someone to a game sends them a notification; an invitation expires after 10 minutes (live games) or a day (turn games), and a game nobody plays for a while ends on its own. Games never count for the Season (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Notifications: we keep a list of updates for you (for example someone wants to meet, said yes, wrote to you, invited you to a game, or is visiting your country). If you turn on notifications, we store your browser’s push address or your phone app’s push token so we can deliver them, and delete it when you turn them off (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

The Season: if you join the Season competition, your name, photo, country and points are shown to other members on the Season board, as described in the Season rules. Points are counted from meetings both people confirmed. If the Season’s prize is a trip, you tell us when you join where you’d like to travel and when; only the Fistbump team sees this, and sponsors see only totals of three or more. We may check the leaders’ accounts, meetings and live photos before announcing a winner, to keep the competition fair (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). If a travel partner pays for the winner’s trip and the winner accepts it, we give the partner only what it needs to book the trip, such as the winner’s name (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Meeting photos: a photo you add of a meeting is stored privately and shown only to the two of you. Others see it on your profiles only if you both approve that photo and you both show who you’ve fistbumped; either of you can withdraw that at any time, and it never shows to people you blocked or who blocked you. We remove location and other metadata from the picture before it is uploaded. Live photos taken with the in-app camera record the pose you were asked for and the time. Photos are deleted with your account (Art. 6(1)(a) and (b) GDPR).

Payments: on the website, Stripe processes your payment for Fistbump Plus. We receive your Stripe customer ID, your plan, its status and when it renews or ends, never your card details. In the iPhone and Android apps, Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) processes it. The app gives the store your Fistbump member ID so the purchase is linked to your account, and we receive from Apple or Google the subscription’s ID, plan, status, renewal and end dates, and whether it was refunded, never your payment details (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; invoices are kept as tax law requires, Art. 6(1)(c)).

Usage: we note when you last opened Fistbump, to count active members, and the app shows how many builders are online right now as a single number, without saying who.

Safety: reports and blocks are used to keep members safe and to act on abuse (legitimate interests, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Who can see what

Only approved members see the member map, and only according to your settings: by default just your X connections who use Fistbump, or also other approved builders if you allow it, or nobody if you hide. Your location is shown at country or city level only. Your mood, your move, your Fistbump news and your Fistbump Park ladder follow the same settings, and who you met and your meeting photos also need the choices described above. Messages are visible only to the two people in the conversation. Exact event venues are shown only to guests who are going, unless the host marks the venue as a public place.

Sponsors who support Fistbump can sign in to a sponsor page. They see only counts: how many builders there are, rough regions rounded to about 100 km, popular interests and destinations. Groups of fewer than three builders are left out and members who chose to be hidden are not counted on the map. Sponsors never see names, photos, handles, messages or exact places.

Travel partner applications: when a company applies at app.fistbump.world/partners, we store the company’s name and website, the contact’s name and work email, and what they wrote, to reply and to prepare an agreement (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Only the Fistbump team sees them. We delete an application on request, and within 12 months if no partnership follows.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores the database and photos and handles sign-in, hosted in the EU (Frankfurt). Our X access tokens are stored encrypted.
  • Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Ireland) processes payments for Fistbump Plus under its own privacy policy.
  • Your browser’s push service (for example Google, Apple or Mozilla) delivers notifications to browsers, and Expo (650 Industries, USA) delivers them to the iPhone and Android app, through Apple’s and Google’s push services. Notification contents are encrypted for browsers.
  • X Corp. provides sign-in and the profile and follow data above, under X’s own privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages serves this website and the web app, and OpenFreeMap serves the map tiles. Both receive your IP address when your browser loads files from them.

Supabase and Expo process personal data on our behalf, under the data processing terms that are part of their standard service agreements. Stripe and X Corp. handle the data they receive under their own privacy policies.

Some of these providers are based in the USA (Expo, X Corp., GitHub) or may process data outside the EU. Where personal data leaves the EU, it is protected by the EU’s standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision (such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework), as set out in each provider’s terms.

Cookies and storage

We don’t use advertising or analytics cookies. Your browser stores your sign-in session and a few settings, such as the game level you chose, so the app keeps working between visits. If you try the map before you sign in, a picture you pick for your character stays in your browser only.

How long we keep data

We keep your account data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, photos, X connection checks, requests, messages, announcements, events, Fistbumps, moods and Fistbump Park ladder and games are deleted. Reports about other members may be kept as long as needed to handle them, and invoices as long as tax law requires (usually 10 years in Germany).

Your rights

Delete your account in the app: your picture (top right) → 🛡️ Privacy → Delete my account. It is deleted at once. If you can’t sign in, see fistbump.world/delete-account/.

Download your data: your picture (top right) → 🛡️ Privacy → Download my data. The file includes your profile, your X connection checks, requests, the messages you sent, trips, events, Fistbumps, Season status, your mood, and your Fistbump Park games, ladder and ladder history.

You can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, a copy in a portable format, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interests. Write to contact@skatalystai.com. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA).

You can disconnect Fistbump from your X account at any time in X’s settings under connected apps.
