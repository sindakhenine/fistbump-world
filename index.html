How it works

  1. Your X people on a map

    See the people you follow, and the people who follow you, who use Fistbump. Each member shows a country or a city, never an address.

  2. Ping for a coffee

    Suggest a coffee, a walk or a build session. When they say yes, a chat opens to set the time and place.

  3. Meet and fistbump

    Take a fistbump photo together and both confirm the meeting. Your fistbumps add up on your profile.

  4. Say where you’ll be

    In Lisbon next week? Tell your followers, the people you follow, or builders near the place.

Also in Fistbump

  • The Season

    Season 1 runs from 15 November 2026 to 31 March 2027 (Berlin time), so meetings from launch day count. Free and Plus earn points the same way. Each new builder you meet in person earns 1 point in your own country, and 8 points for the one who travelled to meet outside their home country. Every fistbump photo is checked. The Season rules

  • Fistbump Park

    Quick games with your connections or against bots, between meetings. Just for fun: games never count for the Season.

  • Privacy and safety

    Every member signs in with their own X account, and only a country or a city is shown. Block or report anyone, and download your data or delete your account at any time.

Fistbump is free. Fistbump Plus is an optional monthly or yearly subscription: meet builders outside your X network, hear when they are in your town, write your own invitations, host small events and play more games. Cancel any time. The first 1,000 members get a Founding member badge; the first 100 members who pay for Plus get a Founding Plus badge, and on the website 20% off the first month of a monthly subscription.
